# 04 — Monetization & Live Operations

## 1. Principles

- **Never sell power.** No stronger weapons, no guaranteed survival, no exclusive better loot.
- **Sell identity and convenience.** Looks, space, speed of non-competitive tasks.
- **Earnable path to most things.** Paying accelerates; it doesn't gatekeep.
- **No paid random items** at launch. If added later, follow Roblox's current policy (odds disclosure and any age restrictions).

## 2. Revenue streams

### 2.1 Game passes (one-time)
| Pass | Suggested price | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Extra Bag Pocket | 149 R$ | +1 carry slot (small; balanced so it's convenience not dominance) |
| Deluxe Base | 199 R$ | +10 display slots, aquarium wall |
| Fast Boat | 99 R$ | Faster surface travel / quicker queue |
| VIP Diver | 299 R$ | VIP hub lounge, name tag color, daily bonus Pearls |

### 2.2 Developer products (repeatable)
- **Pearl packs** (premium currency for cosmetics): 80 / 400 / 1,000 / 2,200 R$ tiers with increasing bonus.
- **Restore relic** (repair a damaged relic's value) — also purchasable with Coins.

### 2.3 Cosmetics (Pearls)
- Diving suits, helmets, lamp colors, bubble trails, sub skins, base decorations, emotes.
- Rotating shop (daily/weekly) creates urgency without randomness.

### 2.4 Expedition Pass (seasonal)
- ~8–10 week seasons, free and premium tracks (~399 R$ premium).
- Rewards: cosmetics, titles, base items, Pearls (enough to partly fund next pass).
- Progress through dives and weekly challenges, which also drives retention.

### 2.5 Other Roblox revenue
- **Engagement-based payouts** from Premium subscribers' playtime (automatic).
- **Rewarded video ads** (if eligible in your region/age settings) for optional bonuses. Check current Roblox ad policies.
- **Private servers** (small monthly price) for squads that want to play alone.

## 3. Pricing tips

- Launch with prices moderate; raise premium items once you see conversion data. Lowering prices later feels bad to earlier buyers.
- Show the shop after the player's first successful extraction, not before; players buy when they're emotionally invested.
- Offer a **starter pack** (one-time, great value) shown once at the end of the first session.

## 4. Economy health

- Track coins **sourced** vs **sunk** daily. If sources outrun sinks for long, prices inflate and progression breaks.
- Sinks: upgrades, repairs, base items, relic restoration, cosmetic dyes.
- Review upgrade costs so a free player reaches Zone 2 in about 3–5 hours of play.

## 5. KPIs for monetization

| Metric | Healthy early target |
|---|---|
| Payer conversion (% of DAU who purchase) | 1–3% |
| ARPDAU | Track trend; improve through content, not pressure |
| Shop view → purchase | Watch per item; remove items nobody buys |
